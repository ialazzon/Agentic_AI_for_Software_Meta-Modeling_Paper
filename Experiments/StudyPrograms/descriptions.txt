The Computer Science Department offers a Software Engineering Programme, which started in 2020 and lasts for 3 years. This programme includes multiple semesters and specialisations. In Fall 2021, students take courses like Introduction to Programming (CS101) and Data Structures (CS102), each worth 7.5 ECTS. In Spring 2022, Algorithms (CS201) is available as a Master-level course with an access level of M1A. The programme offers a specialisation in Artificial Intelligence, beginning in Semester 3, where students study courses like Machine Learning (AI301) in Fall 2023. The AI specialisation includes additional semesters and courses, ensuring a focused learning path. Beyond the programme, the department also offers Cybersecurity Fundamentals (CS999), a Bachelor-level course available in both Fall and Spring semesters.
