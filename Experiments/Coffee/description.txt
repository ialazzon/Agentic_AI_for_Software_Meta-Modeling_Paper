The EspressoMaster3000 coffee machine consists of a Control Unit, Brewing Unit, Water Tank, and Dip Tray. The Control Unit includes a processor with a clock speed of 3200 MHz and 4 cores, an 8GB RAM, and a display with a resolution of 128x64. The machine has a workflow with three tasks: an automatic task GrindBeans, which lasts 5 seconds and uses the Brewing Unit, another automatic task Brewing that takes 10 seconds, and a manual task Pour Coffee, which lasts 3 seconds.
