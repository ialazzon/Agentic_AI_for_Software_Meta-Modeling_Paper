The City Library contains three writers: Agatha Christie, Isaac Asimov, and Walter Isaacson. Agatha Christie has written two books, Murder on the Orient Express and The A.B.C. Murders, both belonging to the Mystery category. Isaac Asimov authored Foundation, which is categorized under Science Fiction, while Walter Isaacson wrote Steve Jobs, a Biography. The library holds a collection of books across different genres, showcasing a diverse range of literature.
